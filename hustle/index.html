How to Hustle

Your goal is to earn **$100** in 2 minutes to pay off your Daily Nebraskan debt! Speed and accuracy are key to pleasing the impatient Husker fans.

1️⃣

Take Order & Manage Patience

Click **"Start Serving"**. Watch the **Patience Meter**—if it runs out, the customer leaves and you lose money!

2️⃣

Grill It Right

Click **"Start Grill"** and then immediately click **"Remove Dog"** when the progress bar is in the **GREEN ZONE** (the sweet spot) for a perfect cook!

3️⃣

Assemble Accurately

Add the toppings in the **EXACT ORDER** listed on the ticket. Get it right, then **"Serve Order"** to get paid!

Game Over

You ran out of time! The fans are going home hungry and angry. Your debt remains unpaid.
